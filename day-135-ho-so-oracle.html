Hồ sơ công ty: Oracle 🗄️

Oracle
Tình huống: Hồ sơ Oracle: Larry Ellison và database quan hệ, đế chế phần mềm doanh nghiệp, mua Sun lấy Java, vào cloud trễ mà vẫn sống khoẻ.
1Larry EllisonLarry Ellison
1977, Larry Ellison lập công ty với hai ngàn đô — đặt tên Software Development Labs.
In 1977, Larry Ellison started the company with two thousand dollars — Software Development Labs.
/ɪn 1977, ˈlæri ˈɛlɪsən ˈstɑrtɪd ðə ˈkʌmpəni wɪð tu ˈθaʊzənd ˈdɑlərz, ˈsɔfˌtwɛr dɪˈvɛləpmənt læbz/

He read an IBM paper on relational databases — and shipped the product before IBM did.
/hi rɛd ən aɪ bi ɛm ˈpeɪpər ɑn rɪˈleɪʃənəl ˈdeɪtəˌbeɪsɪz, ænd ʃɪpt ðə ˈprɑdəkt bɪˈfɔr aɪ bi ɛm dɪd/

He renamed it Oracle after a CIA project he'd worked on — sounds mystical.
/hi riˈneɪmd ɪt ˈɔrəkəl ˈɑftər ə si aɪ eɪ ˈprɑʤɛkt hid wɜrkt ɑn, saʊndz ˈmɪstɪkəl/
Khách đầu tiên: CIA — không đùa đâu.
First customer: the CIA — no joke.
/fɜrst ˈkʌstəmər, ðə si aɪ eɪ, noʊ ʤoʊk/

Oracle Database became the standard for big business — banks, telcos, governments.
/ˈɔrəkəl ˈdeɪtəˌbeɪs bɪˈkeɪm ðə ˈstændərd fər bɪɡ ˈbɪznəs, bæŋks, ˈtɛlkoʊz, ˈɡʌvərnmənts/

Once you run on Oracle, you can't leave — textbook lock-in.
/wʌns ju rʌn ɑn ˈɔrəkəl, ju kænt liv, ˈtɛkstˌbʊk lɑk ɪn/

/ˈlaɪsənsɪz plʌs səˈpɔrt, ˈmʌni ˈroʊlɪŋ ɪn ˈɛvri jɪr, ə ˈɡoʊldən kæʃ floʊ/
Sales Oracle nổi tiếng hung hãn — văn hoá 'win at all costs'.
Oracle sales are famously aggressive — a 'win at all costs' culture.
/ˈɔrəkəl seɪlz ɑr ˈfeɪməsli əˈɡrɛsɪv, ə wɪn æt ɔl kɔsts ˈkʌltʃər/

In 2010 they grabbed Sun Microsystems — landing Java and MySQL.
